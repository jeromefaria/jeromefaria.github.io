biography

 

NNY is the recording and performing alias of Jerome Faria (b. 1983), a media artist from Portugal..

 

His music is described as “a complex experiment in sound dissection, as Jerome pursues pure forms of sound composition, some being harsh and brute while others are harmonious and contemplating.”

 

His work has been published under a variety of formats including book, CD, DVD, live performance and installation across Europe, Asia and the United States.
