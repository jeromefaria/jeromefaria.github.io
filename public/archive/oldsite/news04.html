december

"Closer to another end of the cycle which nearly completes five years in time of existence. looking back takes us to a year filled with public exposure of the consequences of dealing with experiences and emotions which result in those pieces of sound and imagery i throw at you.

Your response have been apreciated and respected. the reason that encourages the continuation of this process is the presence you give it.
as new items are taking shape, i leave you with a taste of visual poetry by Ed Emshwiller "Thanatopsis" accompanied by a soundscape of my own.

Best wishes for Two Thousand Five.
- JF

november

- Done mixing the 3 tracks recorded with Extended in September at Paulo Ferraz Studio. The band and JF are happy about the final product. The songs will air soon on Antena 3 prior to the final gig that will take place next November 20th at Madeira Tecnopolo along with Karnak Seti , Dogma 4.1 and Andre Indiana.
