- NNY will contribute a new track to the compilation "Falésia" which is scheduled to be released on June 2nd on Enough Records with a release party at 22h that night taking place at Fábrica De Som featuring live act's by Stalker Vitki, VelgeNaturlig and ps. - Recently I was interviewed by MadeiraByNight.com about the new album NNY++. You can read the full interview (in portuguese) here.

- Since I don't have any pictures of the Lisbon show at Crew Hassan, I thought I would share an audio recording of the set I performed that night.

The file is in MP3 format compressed at 196kbps and has a size of 93mb.

The set is one hour long and contains old music as well as sounds from the new album "NNY++" and some new unreleased tracks as well.

Bare in mind that this recording is protected under a Creative Commons License and you cannot make commercial use nor modify the recording. As long as you attribute the work in the way that it has been handed to you by the author (that would be me), then you are free to copy, share and distribute it in any way you like.

All that said, you can download the file for free here.

Last but not least, thanks to everyone that showed up, I had a great time playing there.

Enjoy.

Jerome
